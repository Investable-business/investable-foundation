Building programmes into Africa's future economy.

Investable Foundation NPC is a South African nonprofit and public benefit organisation expanding access to economic opportunity for young people and entrepreneurs.

Our mission

Practical capabilities, networks, mentorship and opportunities.

We expand access to economic opportunity by equipping beneficiaries with what supports sustainable livelihoods and meaningful economic participation.

Registered in 2026. Delivering since 2020. Our team has run Founder Institute South Africa since 2020, mostly pro bono: nine cohorts and more than 90 alumni. We registered Investable Foundation NPC to give that work a permanent public-benefit home, and to bring the same discipline to young people preparing for work and life after school.

How we work.

  • Coordinated from South AfricaOur founders, mentors and partners span 13 African countries. We set the programme standard centrally and work with people on the ground who understand local customers, laws and routes to market.
  • Remote firstHybrid and in-person activities are delivered project by project using contracted venues. That keeps fixed overheads low and directs resources to programme delivery.
  • Specialist facultyThe Foundation designs and coordinates the programme, participant journey, quality and outcomes. Formal partners in legal, technology, marketing, compliance and impact measurement deliver where required.
