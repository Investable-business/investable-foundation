Questions about programmes.

What is active venture development?

Hands-on, stage-gated support for founders with early traction. We work inside the business on sales, pricing, contracts, systems and AI until the next milestone is met. It is not shared office space or a few check-in meetings.

Do founders have to go through Founder Institute first?

Our founder pipeline starts with Founder Institute South Africa. Founders who complete it, or are active in it, can enter active venture development. It is a quality signal, not a gate.

Can my company fund a programme from its Skills Development, ED or CSI budget?

Yes. We co-design programmes around your mandate and the outcomes you want, then deliver with our specialist faculty. Investable Foundation is a Section 18A approved PBO.

Who delivers the programmes?

The Foundation designs and holds the programme framework. Delivery is led by a curated specialist faculty of domain experts in AI, sales, legal, marketing and impact.
