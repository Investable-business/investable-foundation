    Questions people ask us.

    Is Investable Foundation a registered nonprofit?

    Yes. We are a registered NPC (2026/031990/08), NPO (334-886-NPO) and SARS-approved PBO (930089484) with Section 18A approval.

    If you registered in 2026, how do you have a track record?

    Our team has delivered Founder Institute South Africa since 2020, across nine cohorts and more than 90 alumni. The NPC gives that work a permanent public-benefit home.

    How do I apply to Founder Institute South Africa?

    Apply at investablefoundation.org/apply. We will share the next cohort dates when you apply.

    Can I get a Section 18A tax certificate for my donation?

    Yes. Tell us when you donate and we will send you a Section 18A receipt. Any cash donation qualifies, including once-off, monthly and corporate donations. Donations of goods or services, and sponsorships that come with a significant benefit in return, may not qualify.

    Do you fund bursaries, students or other charities?

    No. We run structured founder development and work-readiness programmes. We do not fund individual bursaries, student studies, other foundations or charities.

    What is venture development?

    Hands-on, stage-gated support for founders with early traction. We work inside the business on sales, pricing, contracts, systems and AI until the next milestone is met. It is not shared office space.
