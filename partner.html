Questions funders ask.

What type of funders does the Foundation work with?

South African corporates deploying B-BBEE spend (Skills Development, Enterprise Development and Supplier Development), international foundations and development finance institutions, and diaspora donors funding specific founders through the Sponsor a Founder model.

What B-BBEE categories does the Foundation qualify for?

Programmes can be structured to qualify under Skills Development (3.5% leviable payroll, plus Section 12H), Enterprise Development (1% NPAT) and Supplier Development (2% NPAT), depending on your company's scorecard and programme design.

Can international donors give in USD or GBP?

Yes. We accept donations in USD and can discuss GBP and EUR options. Donors using donor advised funds, charitable foundations or payroll giving schemes should reach out to discuss how to direct funds to a South African NPO.

What evidence of outcomes does the Foundation provide?

Founder-level outcomes: customer interviews conducted, first paying customers secured, revenue milestones, programme completion and progression. Impact reports can align with IRIS+ metrics on request.

What is the minimum funding commitment?

Sponsor a Founder: R29,999 per founder per 90-day cycle. Programme partnerships: contact us to discuss your mandate and we will design an appropriate programme structure.
